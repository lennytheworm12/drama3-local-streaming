Quick question. Do you know how a toilet works?
Of course you do. You use one several times a day. So on a scale of one to seven, how well do you understand how it works? Give yourself a number. I'm guessing you went with a five or a six. It's a toilet. It's not exactly a mystery.

If you're anything like a lot of people, something strange just happened. A second ago you understood toilets. Now you're standing in front of an imaginary one, and the confidence has quietly left the room.
